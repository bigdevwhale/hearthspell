The old master has gone travelling and left his tower to you, his apprentice. The tower is your home: every floor is a room, every chore is a spell. Cast them and the floors shine. Forget one and harmless imps move in. Master your spells and little helpers wake up. Free, no ads, no account, works offline.

🧹 MADE FOR CHORES THAT AREN'T DAILY
You water the plants every four days, change the bed linen every two weeks and clean the oven once a moon. A checklist that resets every morning is the wrong shape for that, and a streak that breaks is the wrong feeling. In Hearthspell every chore keeps its own rhythm. Its freshness fades slowly from bright to dusty, and the room fades with it. Do the chore, tap the spell, and the room shines again. Nothing burns down and nobody scolds you.

🏰 THE TOWER
• 11 kinds of room, each a pixel scene: the kitchen is the Alchemy Lab, the bedroom an Observatory, the bathroom the Spring Baths
• 45 ready-made spells with sensible intervals, or your own with any name and rhythm
• Pick the day a chore is due next, so it keeps to your week
• Imps instead of punishment: cheeky, harmless, gone with one cast
• 17 helpers wake up as you master their spells and pace the floors
• Mana for every chore, spent on decor for the tower and robes for the apprentice
• The master's three requests each week are worth double mana
• Low on energy? One button shows only the small spells

📜 THE REST OF THE HOUSE
• Letters from the master: twelve of them, one a week, from his travels
• The market: a shopping list sorted into shop aisles that learns what runs low
• A bestiary of eighteen named imps, a star chart of twelve constellations, gifts left by the helpers, potions, Sunday tea
• Seasons: autumn leaves, Hallowe'en pumpkins, winter snow, spring petals
• One quiet reminder a day, only when imps have moved in or a letter has arrived
• A home-screen widget with the tower from outside

🌙 QUIET BY DESIGN
No ads, no account, no analytics and no internet access. Everything stays on your phone, and Settings → Backup saves it all to one file you keep.
